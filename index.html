05 / POLICE & COMMUNITY WORKFLOW

Better decisions begin
with better context.

CrimeMap is a concept for responsible, evidence-led public-safety planning.

STEP 01
▤

Collect verified reports

Use approved records with clear timestamps, locations, access controls and source notes.

STEP 02
⌖

Review spatial patterns

Compare time periods and areas; check for reporting bias and incomplete data.

STEP 03
↗

Coordinate proportionately

Use professional review to plan coverage, then evaluate outcomes and community impact.

◎ RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Area-level patterns are not proof that an individual has committed a crime. Avoid profiling people or publishing personal information.
